## **Visitor Information**
The monument is accessible year-round but visiting between October and April lets you avoid the intense summer heat. As it's relatively remote with no visitor center or restaurants available, come prepared with enough water, food, and basic amenities. Also note that while there are no formal campgrounds in Castle Mountains, backcountry camping is permitted if you have the necessary permit.
