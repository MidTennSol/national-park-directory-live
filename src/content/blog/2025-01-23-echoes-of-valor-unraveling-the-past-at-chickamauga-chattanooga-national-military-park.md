Welcome to a land where nature holds hands with history, painting a landscape both stunningly beautiful and deeply significant. Sprawling across two states - Georgia and Tennessee - the Chickamauga & Chattanooga National Military Park serves as a living testament to some of the most fierce Civil War battles fought amidst rolling hills and mighty ridges. This park isn’t just about war; it offers an array of recreational activities amid its verdant expanses while keeping the spirit of remembrance alive.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

Established in 1890 primarily to preserve and commemorate battlefields related to the American Civil War, this park tells tales of bravery, sacrifice, victory, and loss. The Battle of Chickamauga in 1863 was one of the deadliest during the war and served as a precursor to another crucial conflict – The Battle for Chattanooga – which ultimately paved way for Sherman’s infamous 'March to the Sea'. 

The geology too has its part in shaping these historic events as it dictated military strategies during those fateful days. The region is mainly characterized by rugged terrain including dominant features like Lookout Mountain and Missionary Ridge that played strategic roles during battles.
