## **Introduction**
Welcome to an adventure into America's past along the El Camino Real de Tierra Adentro National Historic Trail. This royal road was once a vital artery linking Mexico City to Santa Fe. Today, it invites visitors to journey back in time and immerse themselves in a centuries-old tapestry of culture and history. 

Stretching over 1,600 miles across New Mexico and Texas, with a footprint reaching into Mexico, this trail offers scenic views, archaeological sites, traditional communities, native flora and fauna, festivals, food, art, storytelling traditions, music performances and so much more.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Traveling El Camino Real is like flipping through pages of North American history. Established by Spanish explorers in the late 16th century as the main trade route between Mexico City and the Spanish colonial capital at Santa Fe, it was later used by explorers, settlers, missionaries and traders for 300 years up until the railroad era.

From a geological perspective too this trail is riveting. It winds its way through diverse landscapes: the rugged Chihuahuan Desert; towering mountain ranges like the Sierra Madre and Sangre de Cristo; the mesmerizing gypsum dune fields of White Sands National Monument; and the fertile banks of Rio Grande that have sustained farming over millenniums.
