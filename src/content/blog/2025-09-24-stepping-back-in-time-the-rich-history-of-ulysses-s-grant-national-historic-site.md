## **Introduction**

Imagine stepping back into mid-19th century Americana as you set foot on a five-acre plot where one of America's most influential figures once resided. This place is none other than the Ulysses S. Grant National Historic Site, located in St. Louis, Missouri. From exploring the main house named "White Haven," wandering around horse stables, or learning about Julia Dent – Grant’s wife and slave master’s daughter - your visit promises a multi-faceted experience that fuses historical richness with natural beauty.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

Originally part of a 1,000-acre plantation, White Haven hosts green lawns today that are remnants of an era filled with intricacies and contradictions. With soil rich from decades of farming and geological deposits from the nearby Mississippi River, it offers a glimpse into plantation life juxtaposed with events that ignited the Civil War.

Most notably, this site was home to Ulysses S. Grant – Union General during the Civil War and later America's 18th President - who married into a slave-owning family yet fought relentlessly against slavery.

## **Activities Guide**

This historic site offers you plenty to explore! Begin by touring White Haven. Inside, exhibits fill each room providing insights into Grant's personal life amidst vivid period architecture and decor that transport you back to the mid-1800s.

Outside, you’ll find the horse stables – a must-see given Grant’s lifelong love for horses. A short walk leads to the slave quarters where four enslaved African Americans once lived, offering a stark perspective into America's past struggle for freedom and equality.

Don't miss out on ranger-led programs or Junior Ranger activities if you're traveling with children - these interactive sessions add depth to your historic journey.

## **Visitor Information**

The site is open Wednesday through Sunday from 9 am to 5 pm, with free admission. Guided tours leave on the hour and last about half an hour. Keep in mind that while the site is wheelchair friendly, some trails outside might present difficulties due to uneven terrain.
 
## **Tips for Different Visitors**

History enthusiasts will marvel at deeply rooted stories of Grant and his era, while nature lovers can stroll along well-manicured lawns. Photographers will appreciate the chance to capture vintage architectural details contrasted by verdant greenery.

Parents should make use of the kid-friendly exhibits, activities, and booklets which aid youngsters in understanding America's past. Additionally, senior visitors can inquire about mobility assistance or audio guides for a more comfortable visit.

## **Regional Context**

Your visit to Ulysses S. Grant National Historic Site is just one part of what St. Louis has to offer. Explore nearby attractions like the Gateway Arch or Forest Park for a complete experience of this vibrant mid-western city.
