## **Historical/Cultural/Geographical Background**

Harmony Hall stands as a testament to America's colonial era. Built around 1769 by Enoch Magruder, a wealthy Maryland merchant and planter, it still maintains an air of grandeur and elegance that takes one back in time. Later being owned by notable figures such as Richard Potts Jr., this mansion witnessed many defining moments of American history firsthand.

With its flamboyant Georgian architecture style featuring charming wooden elements and a wide entrance hall that opens onto vast green lawns – surrounded by age-old trees offering dappled shade – Harmony Hall stands proudly along Broad Creek. Its proximity to both the Potomac River and central Washington D.C set it apart amidst other heritage sites.
