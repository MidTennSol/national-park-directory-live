## **Introduction**
Welcome to one of America’s most unique national parks- the War in the Pacific National Historical Park. This spectacular park straddles two worlds- one steeped in raw natural beauty, and another enfolding stories from one of mankind's intense wars. On this Pacific Island paradise, you'll tread along pristine beaches once scarred by war machines and explore lush jungles which echo the tales of soldiers who fought bravely.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
The park is a living testament to WWII with numerous memorials scattered across its serene landscape. It was established on August 18th, 1978 to commemorate military events that took place in the Mariana Islands during WWII, including the Battle for Guam from July 21 – August 10, 1944. Spread out over several locations on Guam’s western shore, each site offers distinct historical vignettes—tank traps at Asan Beach, Japanese coastal defense guns at Ga'an Point or remnants of Piti Coastal Defense Guns- whispering poignant stories about humanity amid chaos.

Guam’s fascinating geological highlights also come alive here as you witness three distinct coral limestone layers from different periods etched across its cliffs while exploring jungle trails. 

## **Activities Guide**
This richly varied park holds plenty to see and do for all ages. Walk down memory lane with a Heritage Tour visiting WWII relics or get acquainted with local marine life in the T. Stell Newman Visitor Center’s exhibits. Take a refreshing hike through verdant jungles filled with beautiful endemic species or snorkel around vibrant coral reefs teeming with tropical fishes.

Join park rangers for an enlightening "Walk in the Park" program which brings to life WWII history, flora, and fauna. Attend special events commemorating significant war dates like July 21st (Liberation Day) and enjoy reenactments, static displays, and oral histories.

## **Visitor Information**
