The Lower Delaware River has shaped America's past in ways that few other rivers have. Reflecting deep historical imprints, it had seen the pivotal crossing by General George Washington during the Revolutionary War - an event forever etched in American memory on Christmas night 1776. 

Moreover, as one navigates along this waterway, they are met with awe-inspiring geological formations dating back to hundreds of millions of years. This river arena exhibits diabase boulders, towering cliffs studded with greenery from prehistorically eras—an encapsulating sight showcasing nature’s magnificence! The river also forms part of an Important Bird Area (IBA) making it as scenically diverse as it is ecologically crucial.
