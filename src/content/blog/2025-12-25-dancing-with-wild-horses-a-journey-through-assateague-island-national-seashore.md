## **Introduction**

Experience a breath of salty air and the gentle touch of sea spray as you step onto the untouched landscapes of Assateague Island National Seashore. Located on Maryland’s Eastern Shore, this tranquil hideaway provides visitors with an immersive exposure to nature that few other places can match. The rhythmic crashing waves coupled with wind-tossed dunes create a soothing melody that serves as background music for exploring this bewitching island.
  
## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

Perhaps Assateague's most famous residents are its majestic wild horses. Rumored descendants of survivors from shipwrecks off the Virginia coast in the 17th century, these horses have adapted to thrive in this harsh environment and have become an inseparable part of the island’s identity.

## **Activities Guide**

The park offers myriad activities that take full advantage of its diverse ecosystems. Enjoy kayaking or canoeing through the serene marshlands; spot herons or snowy egrets while bicycling along well-groomed paths; or grab your binoculars to witness the migratory bird spectacle during spring and fall.

## **Visitor Information**

The park is open all year long. However, summer and early fall offer the most conducive conditions for outdoor activities. Remember that facilities like restrooms and campsites are limited, so plan ahead. The Assateague Island visitor centers (one in Maryland and one in Virginia), provide numerous exhibits, films, and ranger-led programs for visitors to fully understand this unique place.

## **Tips for Different Visitors**

Birdwatchers should visit during migration season (spring & fall) for a potential sighting of over 300 species of birds. Beach-lovers can enjoy sunbathing along Assateague's magnificent beach, although they should be aware that lifeguards are stationed only at designated locations.

Families with children will appreciate Assateague’s Junior Ranger Program, which encourages kids to learn about conservation while exploring nature through hands-on activities. Those interested in history should not miss a trip to the Assateague Lighthouse on the Virginia side of the island.
