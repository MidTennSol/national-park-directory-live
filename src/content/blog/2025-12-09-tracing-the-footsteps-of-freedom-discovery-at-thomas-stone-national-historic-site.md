# Tracing the Footsteps of Freedom: Discovery at Thomas Stone National Historic Site

*Unearth a tapestry of American history tucked away in the rolling landscapes of Southern Maryland. Visit the Thomas Stone National Historic Site and rekindle centuries-old stories of liberty, rebellion, and resilience.*

## **Introduction**
Welcome, fellow explorers! Today we venture to a hidden gem gracefully nestled within the bucolic serenity of Charles County, Maryland. A mere 25 miles south of Washington D.C., the Thomas Stone National Historic Site is more than an exquisitely preserved plantation home - it's a journey back in time to revolutionary America.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
A signatory of the Declaration of Independence, Thomas Stone plays a crucial role in America's founding tale. The habitation built by him, popularly known as "Haberdeventure," has stood for over two centuries as a sentinel to his life and service. This Georgian-style colonial manor encapsulates his ethos and intimately reflects upon the way early Americans lived.

Constructed on 322 acres in 1771, this estate offers visitors a vivid insight into plantation culture during its peak years and the nuanced lifestyle it fostered. Immerse yourself amid emerald-green pastures dotted with wildflowers that bloom to charm every season while palisade forests guard this tranquil setting—a scenic reminder that beauty can bloom even amidst historical turmoil.

## **Activities Guide**
When you step onto Haberdeventure’s grounds, expect to journey through time via myriad activities tailored to satisfy curious minds and adventurous spirits alike. Commence your expedition with guided tours narrated by knowledgeable rangers. Each room speaks volumes about Thomas Stone's family as various artifacts, paintings, and furnishings revive historic narratives.

Stop by the Visitor Center that hosts intriguing exhibits or explore some captivating films illustrating America's early days. If you're looking to bask in nature's serenity, wander around the picturesque grounds dotted with footpaths leading to tranquil picnic spots or the family cemetery.

## **Visitor Information**
The site operates from Wednesday through Sunday, 9 am – 5 pm. While admission is free of charge, guided tours cost around $3 per adult. It is inclusive for America the Beautiful National Park passes.

Accessibility provisions include wheelchair-accessible amenities and facilities: restrooms, visitor center, and picnic area. However, owing to its historical nature, Haberdeventure house has limited accessibility.

## **Tips for Different Visitors**
History aficionados will appreciate the detailed exhibits in the Visitor Center and the knowledgeable guided tours. Nature lovers can explore trails crisscrossing through breathtaking natural landscapes adorned with native flora and fauna.

For those traveling with children, a junior ranger program available at this site makes history interactive and fun! 

Photographers - don't forget your cameras! The historic manor framed against fiery sunset hues creates stunning visuals you wouldn’t want to miss capturing!
