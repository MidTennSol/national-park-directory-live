Steeped in history, blessed by Mother Nature, and offering a spectrum of activities for explorers- Great Egg Harbor River invites you to indulge your spirit of adventure. This 129-mile long river system, recognized in 1992 as part of the National Wild and Scenic Rivers System, is more than just flowing water—it’s an immersive experience into tranquility draped in verdant foliage and teeming wildlife.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Named by Dutch explorers who found egg-shaped stones along its banks—hence 'Egg Harbour,' this river bears witness to millennia past. With more than 5,400 years of human occupancy dating back to ancient indigenous communities who first settled here to the present-day locals who consider it no less than a retreat from bustling city life—every bend and curve speaks volumes about its rich heritage.

The geology that carved open this stunningly beautiful waterway dates back millions of years when glacial events altered the landscape dramatically. Today, it hosts one of the most diverse ecosystems on the East Coast of America with dragonsflies buzzing above its marshlands and eels slithering between grass beds within its depths.
