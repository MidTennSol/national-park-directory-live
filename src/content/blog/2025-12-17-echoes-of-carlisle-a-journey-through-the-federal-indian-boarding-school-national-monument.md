## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Initially conceived by Henry Richard Pratt as an effort toward bridging cultural chasms between Native Americans and Europeans, _Carlisle_ symbolized a melting pot experiment where indigenous children were taught Euro-American customs to "Kill the Indian..save the man". The school carried out forced assimilation practices causing significant cultural dislocation amongst native communities.

The red-brick structures standing today are staunch reminders of these complex narratives. As you venture deeper into this space, it opens up channels to engage with native history - both painful and persevering - nurturing an understanding beyond textbook descriptions or Hollywood clichés.
