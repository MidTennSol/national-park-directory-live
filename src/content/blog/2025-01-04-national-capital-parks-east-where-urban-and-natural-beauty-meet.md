## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

The genesis of these parks can be traced back to 1791 when President George Washington commissioned French architect Pierre L'Enfant to design a city that boasted expansive public lands. Today, these public spaces provide insight into African American heritage and Civil War history among other enlightening themes.

One key site within these parks is Anacostia Park, one of America’s oldest national parks for urban recreation founded over a century ago. Additionally, Fort Dupont Park stands testament to the fort lines set up during the Civil War to protect the capital. Rich with cultural significance is also Frederick Douglass's home ‘Cedar Hill’, casting light on his enduring struggle against slavery.
