## **Introduction**

Welcome to the hallowed halls of Gloria Dei Church National Historic Site nestled within vibrant Philadelphia. ## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

Constructed between 1698-1700 by Swedish settlers along the Delaware River, Gloria Dei Church is claimed as the oldest church building in Pennsylvania and possibly even the entire United States. Despite its Swedish beginnings, it became an Episcopal establishment in 1845 and remains so today.

Structurally, this brick-and-mortar edifice showcases European architectural influences with Gothic-style windows adorning its thick walls. The churchyard is a picturesque sight with scattered tombstones bearing names from centuries ago amidst sweeping trees adding to its antiquated ambiance.
