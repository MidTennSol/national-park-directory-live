## **Introduction**
Welcome to one of the most charming corners of Charlottesville, Virginia! Nestled amidst rolling hills and vast greenery lies the historic district of Green Springs – a national treasure with a profound historical backdrop, rich agricultural landscapes, and rustic tranquility.

Green Springs National Historic Landmark District stretches over 14,000 acres along Route 15 in Louisa County. This area has seen centuries pass by but remains virtually unaltered.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Green Springs' historical roots go deep. The land was once home to native American Indians from different tribes such as the Monacan and Manahoac. However, its true notability comes from the Revolutionary War period when 'Cavalier' settlers made a significant mark on this land.

They brought with them revolutionary farming techniques involving crop rotations that replenished soil nutrients instead of depleting them. This innovative approach led to incredible fertility throughout Green Springs - giving its distinctive "green" identity.

The locale's impressive collection of architecturally-significant homes narrates tales from another era. These buildings range from Georgian mansions built during colonial times to Victorian-style estates that underscored America's Gilded Age.
