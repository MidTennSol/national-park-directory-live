## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Stepping into the Federal Hall National Memorial is like being transported back to some of America’s most decisive moments. The original structure served as New York City's first City Hall before becoming the nation's first Capitol Building under the Constitution in 1789. Here George Washington was inaugurated as President, shaping his vision for a democratic future in what was dubbed "the room where it happened". 

Later reconstructed in 1842 following its previous demolition due to structural instability, this Greek Revival building now hosts exhibits that detail momentous historical events. These include the drafting of The Bill of Rights, regulation of Wall Street after severe financial panics and more.
