## **Introduction**
Immerse yourself in the untouched beauty where water dances alongside lush riverbanks and unique wildlife flourish freely; welcome to the Missouri National Recreational River. Fanning across two states, South Dakota and Nebraska, this national treasure encapsulates 100 miles of pure natural delight - a haven bound to evoke awe in its visitors.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
The Missouri National Recreational River is more than just a pristine wilderness – it's a historical landmark steeped with stories. The area bears strong cultural significance for Native American tribes like Omaha, Ponca, and Yankton Sioux who revere it as ancestral grounds. Early European explorers such as Lewis & Clark navigated these waters, leaving behind a rich tapestry of tales that echo through time.

Geologically speaking, this rippling ribbon of water served as the main sculpting tool carving out the landforms we see today. Glaciers retreated millennia ago leaving a fertile valley that has been gradually shaped by the river's currents. Today, sandbars provide habitat for piping plovers while tall cottonwood trees reach for the sky along its banks – each telling their own tale in this geological saga.
