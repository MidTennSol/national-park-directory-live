## **Introduction**
Standing solemnly on the tranquil banks of Hudson River is a monolithic marvel that narrates the tale of an American hero - the General Grant National Memorial. Known to locals as "Grant’s Tomb," this neoclassical mausoleum houses the mortal remains of famed Civil War general and 18th U.S. President, Ulysses S. Grant, alongside his wife, Julia Dent Grant. Join us as we delve into the magnificence surrounding this iconic monument nestled within New York City's vibrant Riverside Park.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Constructed between 1891 and 1897 at an astonishing cost for its time ($600,000), the memorial was designed by architect John H Duncan. Drawing inspiration from classical mausoleums like Halicarnassus' Mausoleum and Rome's Pantheon, it exhibits elements of Greek Doric and Ionic styles blended with modernized architecture.

The symmetry-rich design details a circular base adorned with ornate bronze doors featuring high-relief depictions of Grant’s life. Crowned by a dome rising over 150 feet high is a long path adorned with intricate mosaics portraying the stars and stripes emblematic of America’s flag.

Guarding this erstwhile commander are statues representing infantry, cavalry, artillery units — a tribute to his valiant leadership during America's Civil War era.

## **Activities Guide**
While visiting the Grant National Memorial, start by marveling at the imposing exterior. The breathtaking architectural grandeur of this final resting place is accompanied by lush green spaces in Riverside Park — an ideal spot for a picnic or tranquil stroll.

Within the memorial, experience facets of President Grant’s life through murals and exhibits. Don’t miss the twin sarcophagi crafted from Wisconsin red granite – a solemn reminder of the couple that rests within these hallowed walls.

Take time to engage with rangers for daily talks. They offer insights into President Grant's military career, presidency, and efforts to ensure civil rights during Reconstruction.

## **Visitor Information**
Open Wednesday through Sunday, 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM, admission to General Grant National Memorial is free year-round. Restroom facilities are available within the visitor center during operating hours. The monument is wheelchair accessible and houses a gift shop boasting souvenirs centered around American history.
