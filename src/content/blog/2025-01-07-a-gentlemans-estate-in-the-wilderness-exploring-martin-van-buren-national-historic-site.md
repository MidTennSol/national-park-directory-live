## **Introduction**

In the rolling hills and tranquil farmland of Kinderhook, New York, just outside Albany, lies an unassuming site steeped in American political history – The Martin Van Buren National Historic Site. This exquisite locale offers visitors not only a glimpse into the life of America's eighth president but also invites them on an enriching journey through a vibrant era in U.S. history that shaped our nation.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

The main attraction here is Lindenwald, a stunning Italianate mansion which was home to one of America's lesser-known presidents - Martin Van Buren after his term ended in 1841. He lived there until his death in 1862. Known as 'Old Kinderhook,' President Van Buren named his 220-acre estate after mighty linden trees surrounding the residence and attributing a nod to his European cultural roots.

Step inside and feel how the house breathes history with many original furnishings from Van Buren's time that give you insights into his illustrious political career and personal life. Here, tales of statecraft intertwine with intimate moments depicting the spirit of mid-19th century country living.

## **Activities Guide**

While exploring Lindenwald is undoubtedly paramount, there are other activities at Martin Van Buren National Historic Site enhancing your historical voyage.

Visitors can take guided tours led by knowledgeable Park Rangers around Lindenwald and experience firsthand the stunning interiors echoing history. The estate's grounds offer a beautiful, peaceful setting perfect for leisurely picnics or crisp afternoon strolls.

The park also provides self-guided tours of the Old Post Road Historic District and 'Wayside Signs,' allowing visitors to journey through the site at their own pace while learning about surrounding points of historical interest.

## **Visitor Information**

Martin Van Buren National Historic Site is free to visit and open throughout the year. However, guided tours of Lindenwald are seasonal from May through October. These tours last approximately one hour and are limited in capacity, so it's recommended to call ahead or check the park's website for tour times.

## **Tips for Different Visitors**

This remarkable site offers something for everyone. History buffs will relish uncovering Van Buren's life and political career, architecture enthusiasts will appreciate Lindenwald’s Italianate design, nature lovers will be enchanted by the verdant landscape perfect for relaxing walks, picnics and photography.

For families visiting with younger children, bring along engaging historical activities or storybooks related to Van Buren or 19th-century America as there are no child-oriented activities onsite. 

For those with mobility issues, note that only the ground floor of Lindenwald is wheelchair accessible.
