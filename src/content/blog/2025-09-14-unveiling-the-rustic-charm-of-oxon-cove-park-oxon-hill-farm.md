## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

The park's origins date back to the 1890s when it was established as an experimental farm for researching livestock productivity. Nestled within its heart is the intriguing Mount Welby home, built around 1807 by Irish immigrant Dr. Samuel DeButts. Deeply influenced by Federal Period architectural styles, Mount Welby speaks volumes about early American domestic life. The home also reverberates with stories of enslaved African Americans who tirelessly worked the land here until emancipation.
