## **Introduction**
Nestled in the picturesque Hill Country of Austin, Texas lies a living testament to American history - The Lyndon B Johnson National Historical Park. This majestic park is a must-visit location for everyone; be it history buffs looking to delve deep into the fascinating chapters of our nation's past, or nature lovers seeking an escape from city life. It offers an intimate look into the life and times of President Lyndon Baines Johnson—his roots, his path to presidency, his triumphs, his tribulations, and his lasting influence on modern-day America.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Lyndon B Johnson National Historical Park is quite literally two parks in one. The eastern half houses the LBJ Ranch, where Johnson spent most of his time during his presidency—the 'Texas White House' as it was known. On this ranch once stood humble beginnings that would shape a presidential legacy. From cattle drives to secret service quarters, every nook and corner whispers tales from the past.

The western half encompasses Johnson City where visitors can discover President Johnson’s childhood home—replete with period furnishings—and the quaint one-room Junction School he attended. Additionally showcasing historic structures like Sam Ealy Johnson Sr.’s log cabin settlement—the president’s grandparent’s home—is a nod to early pioneer living.

## **Activities Guide**
Whether you're exploring individually or on guided tours led by knowledgeable park rangers, there's no shortage of engaging activities. Tour the LBJ Ranch and witness the hangar housing Johnson’s 'Air Force One-Half'. Pay a visit to the President's childhood home or participate in ranger-led talks about Johnson’s lasting political legacy.

Nature enthusiasts can explore the park’s diverse landscape that includes woodlands, grasslands, riverbanks, and wildlife viewing spots. Birdwatchers will be delighted to spot numerous species including the colorful painted bunting and the graceful great blue heron.

## **Visitor Information**
The Lyndon B Johnson National Historical Park is open throughout the year except for Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day. Visitors can download a free driving tour app that provides detailed narrations of various historic sites throughout the park. It also has braille books available for visually impaired guests along with a ranger audio-described tour of exhibits.

## **Tips for Different Visitors**
For families: Plan for at least half a day to fully enjoy what this park offers. Be sure to check out ranger programs made especially for kids!

For history buffs: Don’t miss perusing through invaluable artifacts housed in state-of-the-art visitor centers located at both ends of the park. 

For nature lovers: Pack your binoculars and hiking boots! The nature trail offers expansive views of Hill Country vista and chances to spot local fauna.
