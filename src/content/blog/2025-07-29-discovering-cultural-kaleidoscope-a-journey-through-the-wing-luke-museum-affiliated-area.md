## **Introduction**
Known for its culturally rich heritage and diverse communities, the city of Seattle offers much to explore for keen travelers. Among its bustling urban landmarks lies a site that stands as a testament to generations of Asian Pacific Americans and their storied past - The Wing Luke Museum Affiliated Area. Nestled in the heart of Chinatown-International District, this museum is more than just a repository of artifacts; it's a cultural crucible where traditions have melded seamlessly over time.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
The story of the Wing Luke Museum begins with its namesake, Wing Luke; an influential Chinese-American civil rights leader who became the first Asian-American to hold elected office in the Pacific Northwest. His vision was instrumental in creating a space that would respect, honor and preserve the rich cultural fabric that forms Seattle's multicultural identity today.

The museum itself is housed in East Kong Yick Building, an edifice that has rooted itself deep into Seattle’s historical timeline since 1910. This century-old structure bearing traces of Romanesque Revival style architecture was once home to countless immigrant families and businesses - a true symbol of hope and dreams for Asian Pacific Americans.

## **Activities Guide**
Stepping into the museum transports visitors on an engaging journey across time and cultures. Its exhibits are meticulously curated to showcase intricate nuances depicting various epochs of struggle, transformation, resilience and success experienced by Asian-Pacific communities.

From fascinating exhibits like 'Honoring our Journey' that details the immigration experience, to 'I am Filipino', where you can explore vibrant Filipino culture, there's something to captivate every visitor. For art aficionados, rotating art exhibits featuring Asian Pacific American artists are true visual treats.

Guided tours of the historic hotel including a visit to the immersive ‘Bruce Lee: His Story, Our History’ exhibit and community meal programs offering tantalizing tastes of diverse Asian cuisines further create enriching experiences at Wing Luke.
