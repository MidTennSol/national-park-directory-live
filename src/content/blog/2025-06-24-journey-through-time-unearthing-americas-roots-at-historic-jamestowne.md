## **Introduction**

Few places on earth offer an immersion into history as richly layered and vivid as Historic Jamestowne. Nestled amidst lush greenery in Colonial National Historical Park in Williamsburg, Virginia, it is a sacred space that whispers tales from the dawn of American civilization. As you journey through this hallowed site, you tread in the footsteps of brave pioneers who dared to dream beyond known horizons.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

Historic Jamestowne is a powerful testament to endurance, survival, and transformation. In 1607, 104 English men and boys embarked on a perilous journey across treacherous seas to establish Jamestown - named after their monarch King James I. Despite waves of disease, famine and conflict with Powhatan Indians, Jamestown survived to become England's first permanent colony in North America.

This historic site invites you to trace four centuries worth narratives etched deep within its soil - from breakthrough archaeological discoveries revealing early colonial life and interactions with local tribes to poignant reflections on slavery in America.

## **Activities Guide**

Historic Jamestowne ensures a dynamic interaction with history through various activities. Roam around James Fort's evocative ruins or explore haunting relics unearthed by archaeologists in the Archaearium museum. Witness replicas of artefacts being produced using traditional methods at the Voorhees Archaearium or be drawn into rousing storytelling sessions by costumed historians of colonial America.

Passionate bird-watchers can follow the riverine trails, while those seeking reflective solitude may find solace in the tranquility of the Tercentenary Monument or at peaceful Jamestown Island.

## **Visitor Information**

Historic Jamestowne is open seven days a week, except on Christmas and New Year's Day. Visitors are advised to check for operating hours updates online due to seasonal changes.
