## **Introduction**  

Welcome to the noble brownstone townhome where Theodore Roosevelt—26th President, prolific author, explorer, soldier, and reformer—took his first breaths. Tucked between towering skyscrapers in New York City’s bustling Manhattan district is a snapshot from another time that stands as a testament to the leader who shaped modern America. Lose yourself between these walls rich with history and echo with tales of courage and unyielding spirit—the Theodore Roosevelt Birthplace National Historic Site.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**  

Opened officially as a national historic site on July 25th, 1962, it's important to note that the current building is not the original structure but a replica reimagined owing to abundant historical data. The original house was demolished in 1916 but rebirthed meticulously after public outcry to preserve Roosevelt’s legacy. Today, five historically restored rooms from Teddy’s childhood recreate an authentic 1865 ambiance interspersed with personal family artifacts and an exhibit gallery showcasing Roosevelt’s eventful life.

## **Activities Guide**  

Start your visit by watching an engaging film about Roosevelt's life before embarking on a guided tour through the restored period rooms where you can immerse yourself in late 19th-century opulence and decor. Do take some time to peruse through Teddy Roosevelt’s uniforms from his Rough Riders days alongside other key memorabilia in the exhibit area. 

Special events, such as the reenactment of Roosevelt’s life by an actor or group readings of his writings, are regular occurrences. Check the site's official calendar to ensure you don't miss these unique experiences.

## **Visitor Information**  

The Theodore Roosevelt Birthplace National Historic Site is open Tuesday through Saturday from 9 am to 5 pm. Admission is free, but you need tickets for guided tours which are offered on a first-come, first-served basis. The birthplace is also wheelchair accessible and provides facilities like restrooms and a bookstore with a vast assortment of books about President Roosevelt.
