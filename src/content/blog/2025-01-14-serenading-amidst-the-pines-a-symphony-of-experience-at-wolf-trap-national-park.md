## **Introduction**
Imagine sitting amidst towering pine trees as stars wink down from the twilight sky. The air is filled with the hum of summer insects and an anticipatory silence among gathered spectators. Suddenly, applause breaks the calm as performers take the stage, their melodies rising over the amphitheater and flowing into nature's orchestra. Welcome to Wolf Trap National Park for Performing Arts.

Situated in Fairfax County, Virginia, just outside of Washington D.C., this park serves as an echo chamber for diverse performances – from classical symphonies & grand operas to popular music concerts & innovative contemporary dance showcases. This unique setting invites patrons not merely to observe but fully immerse themselves in a multisensory experience where artistry meets nature.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
The land that is now Wolf Trap began its history as a colonial farmland in 1734 before evolving into a recreational space thanks to Catherine Filene Shouse. Intrigued by the idea of blending nature, performing arts, and cultural experiences, she gifted 100 acres along with funds for constructing an open-air theater to the American people in 1966.

The creation of this special entity led Congress to establish it as a National Park for Performing Arts - showing their commitment towards cultural development alongside preserving natural treasures. Here you can not only appreciate breathtaking performances but also take pleasure from beautifully sculpted gardens, peaceful woodland trails, & occasional wildlife sightings.

## **Activities Guide**

Music may be the star attraction, but Wolf Trap offers more than melodic experiences. Attendees can picnic under lush trees before the show begins or explore the region's flora and fauna on hiking trails that wander through calming woods. Children can enjoy Theatre-in-the-Woods, a fun-filled program from late June to early August featuring puppetry, dance, music & storytelling.

For those artistically inclined, Wolf Trap Opera offers training programs for emerging professionals while its educational initiatives reach over 90,000 children and adults every year.

## **Visitor Information**
From May to September, the Filene Center serves as Wolf Trap's main stage with over 90 shows annually offering a variety of genres. If you prefer cozy indoor performances in an intimate setting, The Barns at Wolf Trap host concerts from October to May.

Tickets for summer performances usually go on sale in late March or early April. While every patron has a seat in the house for ticketed events, Lawn tickets offer flexibility to find your ideal listening spot amidst nature.
