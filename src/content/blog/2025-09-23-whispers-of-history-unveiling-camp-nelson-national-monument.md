## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Established in 1863 as a Union supply depot and hospital during the Civil War, Camp Nelson morphed into something bigger – a pivotal site for African American social advancement. The camp was declared the nation's third-largest recruitment center for African American soldiers towards war end. A place marked by struggle and triumph, these brave men obtained freedom here for themselves and their families under Emancipation legislation.

The geological background is equally mesmerizing. Enveloped in karst landscape characterized by sinking streams, caverns and limestone pavement; its natural setting only enhances its cultural appeal making it an epitome of preserved history juxtaposed on an enchanting terrain.
