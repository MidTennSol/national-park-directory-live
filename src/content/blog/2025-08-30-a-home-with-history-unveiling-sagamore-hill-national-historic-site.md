## **Introduction**
Nestled on the north shore of Long Island lies a remarkable testament to American history – Sagamore Hill National Historic Site. Often referred to as Theodore Roosevelt's "Summer White House". This historical gem gives an intimate look into how one of America's most influential families lived in the 20th century. Rich with personal memorabilia and packed full of artifacts collected by Roosevelt himself throughout his global travels, this site provides more than just a trip down memory lane—it offers an immersive journey through time.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Sagamore Hill stands as a monument to Theodore Roosevelt's colorful life. Constructed in 1884 as a haven for his beloved family, it later morphed into a bustling hub where state matters were often tackled. Here, you’ll explore authentic rooms left virtually untouched since the time of his death in 1919 - from 'TR’s’ book-lined library filled with hunting trophies to Edith Roosevelt's music room adorned with delicate china collections.

Theodore’s passion for nature conservation reflects in the surrounding natural beauty. The exquisite landscape, designed by Frederick Law Olmsted Jr., is drenched in picturesque wonderment— seeping marshes, densely wooded patches, tranquil salt meadows and rolling hills stretching wildly under clear blue skies.

## **Activities Guide**
Whether your interests lie in historic exploration or outdoor adventure, Sagamore Hill has something for everyone. Embark on ranger-led tours through the Roosevelt Home museum, uncover hidden tales held within its walls. Or, dive into the Theodore Roosevelt Museum at Old Orchard, once home to Roosevelt's eldest son which now portrays vivid illustrations of his dynamic public career.

Outdoor enthusiasts can venture on nature walks through the Nature Trail, boasting over 80 acres of diverse habitat and self-guided tours through the beautiful Sagamore Hill grounds. Wildlife sightings are common—keep your eyes peeled for box turtles, red-tail hawks or white-tailed deer!

## **Visitor Information**
Sagamore Hill is open year-round from Wednesday to Sunday (9 a.m. – 5 p.m.), with guided tours available every hour from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. It’s recommended to reserve tickets online beforehand during busier seasons (May - October). The accessible property also provides wheelchairs on request and allows service pets only due to its delicate ecosystem.

## **Tips for Different Visitors**
Visitors who seek solitude will find it best in the early morning or late afternoon when crowds are thinner. For families with children, visit during summer vacation when special children-oriented programs take place at the site, allowing them to learn about nature and history in an engaging manner. Photographers should not miss golden hour—the serene beauty of Sagamore Hill bathes in warm sunset hues.

## **Regional Context**
Located less than an hour from Manhattan by car, Sagamore Hill is easily accessible yet a world away from urban hustle. Its close proximity makes it perfect for day-trips or paired visits alongside nearby attractions like Planting Fields Arboretum State Historic Park and Oyster Bay National Wildlife Refuge for an extended dose of history and nature.
