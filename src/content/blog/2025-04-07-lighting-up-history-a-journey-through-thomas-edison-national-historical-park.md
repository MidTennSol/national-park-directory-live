## **CONTENT**

## **Introduction**
Imagine stepping back in time, not just any time but to an era when some of humanity's most significant inventions were still in their infancy. Welcome to Thomas Edison National Historical Park (NHP) in West Orange, New Jersey! This park is not your typical natural retreat; it's a living testament to human innovation and perseverance featuring over half a million items from one of history's greatest inventors - Thomas Alva Edison.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Established in 1962, the park preserves and displays two main sites: Glenmont, the grand mansion where the Edisons lived for over forty years, resting peacefully amongst thirteen acres of serene landscape; and the Laboratory Complex, an epicenter of creativity where innovations like phonographs and concrete houses took shape. It is here within these red-brick buildings that we unravel Edison's remarkable journey spanning five decades of ceaseless innovation.

## **Activities Guide**
*Feed Your Mind:* Start with touring the Laboratory Complex armed with self-guided audio tours; they depict stories about specific inventions and processes used by Edison. Don't miss out on seeing Black Maria, regarded as America’s first movie studio!

*Glenmont Estate:* The vintage shadow of Glenmont, with its Queen Anne-style architecture, offers a peek into the domestic life of the Edisons. 

*Chemistry Lab:* The Chemistry Lab, a treasure trove of artifacts, showcases an array of chemicals and apparatus used in Edison's research.

*Special Events:* Participate in various events like the Annual Car Show, Glenmont Garden Party and Vintage Base Ball Game that ensure an enriching cultural experience alongside history.

## **Visitor Information**
The park is open Wednesday through Sunday from 10 am to 4 pm. It’s important to note that you need separate tickets for Laboratory Complex and Glenmont Estate tours and they are not sold at the sites but at the Visitor Center or online. Also, remember that photography is permitted only without flash and tripods are not allowed inside buildings.

## **Tips for Different Visitors**
For school groups or families with children, hands-on science activities at the activity center provide a fun way to learn about Edison's inventions. Seniors would appreciate the Ranger-led or audio tours that delve deep into Edison's life. Disabled visitors can access most areas of the park as well as availability of wheelchairs upon request.

## **Regional Context**
Nestled amidst urban sprawl, Thomas Edison NHP provides a link between American innovation history and modern technology while simultaneously offering much-needed green space. Nearby South Mountain Reservation provides hiking trails and waterfalls for nature lovers while Newark Museum houses art collections worth exploring. 

## **Conclusion**
To visit Thomas Edison NHP is to witness a testament to man's indomitable spirit. It provides a vibrant depiction of one man’s contribution to shaping our world - bridging yesterday with tomorrow using threads of ingenuity.
