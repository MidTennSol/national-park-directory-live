## **Introduction**

Imagine stepping back into an era of grandeur and grace, right in the heart of bustling modern-day New York City. At the Theodore Roosevelt Birthplace National Historic Site, you can do just that. This reconstructed brownstone nestled amid towering skyscrapers offers a rare glimpse into the formative years of America’s 26th President – Theodore “Teddy” Roosevelt. 

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

Born in 1858, Teddy Roosevelt was a native New Yorker through and through. His family home stood on this very site until it was demolished in 1916. Thanks to public demand, the house was later recreated to its former glory by combining period pieces and true-to-time replicas. As you step inside, you'll find yourself transported to the mid-19th century, with all its charm intact.

Packed into five fully furnished floors are exhibits that tell compelling stories about Teddy's uniquely inspirational life and journey to American presidency. Whether it's his childhood bedroom with original toys or his father's library, each room holds purposeful reminders of young Teddy’s world.

## **Activities Guide**

The site offers guided tours led by knowledgeable rangers who share captivating anecdotes about Teddy’s early life and character development. Tours begin every hour and last about forty minutes - ideal for both history enthusiasts and curious travelers alike!

Beyond guided tours, permanent exhibitions showcase unique artifacts from Roosevelt’s life. Don't miss 'Man in the Arena' which explores his path from sickly child to Rough Rider, conservationist and President. Additionally, the site hosts occasional special exhibits spotlighting facets of Roosevelt’s influential career and personal life.

## **Visitor Information**

The Theodore Roosevelt Birthplace National Historic Site is open from Wednesday through Sunday, with visiting hours from 9 am to 5 pm. Access to the site is free of charge but guided tours require a small fee. It's centrally located in Manhattan on East 20th Street between Broadway and Park Avenue South – just a stone's throw away from the charming Union Square.

## **Tips for Different Visitors**

Family visitors will appreciate the hands-on educational centers and interactive displays at the museum, which make history come alive for the young ones. History buffs should plan their visit around any ongoing special exhibitions for an added depth of experience. For those seeking tranquility amid urban bustle, the little-known garden at the rear also offers a quiet space to reflect.
