## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

 
This 3,392-acre park encompasses historical gemstones like the Freeman Farm Overlook illustrating the fiercest fightback during Battle of Freeman's farm; Neilson House served as General Horatio Gates’ headquarters; Stark’s Knob interpreting geological saga dating back 450 million years ago; and Victory Woods denoting final encampment spot for British General John Burgoyne before he surrendered on October 17th, 1777.

While treading upon these hallowed grounds, let your mind drift off to those bygone epochs when liberty was more than just a word—it was an aspiration worth fighting for.

## **Activities Guide**
There's more than history waiting for discovery at Saratoga. Nature lovers can explore hiking trails with breathtaking panoramic views that unveil the Hudson River’s serpentine grace, dense woodlands echoing bird songs, and view points offering colorful sunset vistas.

History enthusiasts can spend an invigorating day moseying along the park's 10-mile self-guided auto tour that takes you through 10 unique stops, each narrating a distinct chapter of the Revolutionary War saga. Don't miss out on Ranger-led programs and guided walks to delve deeper into the area's rich history.
