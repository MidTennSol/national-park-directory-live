## **Introduction**

The sprawling plains of Montana are home to one of America's most vivid historical sites - The Grant-Kohrs Ranch National Historic Site. As you travel along the meandering Clark Fork River, you'll enter a world which harks back to an illustrious chapter in American history – The advent of cowboys and cattle barons. A visit here transcends regular sightseeing experiences as it offers an interactive exploration of our country’s rich past.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

Established in 1972, the vast expanse of Grant-Kohrs Ranch safeguards not just a landscape but an epic saga engraved in America's western expansion. It began with Johnny Grant, a rugged entrepreneur who started a small trading business amidst Montana's wilderness. Later, Conrad Kohrs continued the legacy by turning this humble establishment into one of the largest cattle ranches spanning across numerous states and Canadian provinces.

The ranch represents not just individual stories but also geological marvels. Nestled amidst Rockies, its fertile terrain has made it an ideal spot for cattle grazing while enriching it with diverse flora and fauna.
