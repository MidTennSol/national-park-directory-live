## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
The journey into Lowell's rich tapestry begins with the birth of the Industrial Revolution in America. In 1821, Boston's merchant class set their eyes upon East Chelmsford's potential and transformed it into what's now known as Lowell - a booming mill town christened after Francis Cabot Lowell. 

With cotton mills sprouting alongside its waterways powered by Merrimack River’s bounty, Lowell turned out to be America’s first planned industrial city. Populated by "mill girls," young women from New England farms looking for work and European immigrants seeking their American Dream, it morphed into a melting pot reflecting diverse cultural traditions.

## **Activities Guide**
Your exploration through the park is filled with engaging activities that bring history to life! Don't miss out on the Tsongas Industrial History Center where interactive exhibits make learning fun for kids.
