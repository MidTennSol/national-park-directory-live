## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Tupelo National Battlefield immortalizes the intense Battle of Tupelo fought over three days in July 1864. The Union troops led by General Andrew J. Smith clashed valiantly with Confederate forces under Lt. General Stephen D. Lee and Major General Nathan Bedford Forrest. The resulting victory for Union forces was crucial in ensuring safe movement for Union vessels on the Mississippi River, an important component in their eventual victory.
