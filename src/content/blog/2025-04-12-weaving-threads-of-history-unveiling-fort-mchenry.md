## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background** 

Fort McHenry was designed after the popular European star fort model used in the 18th century. Named after James McHenry, a distinguished statesman and then Secretary of War under President George Washington's administration, Fort McHenry followed his vision for a unique defense line against naval threats.

The fort gained fame during the Battle of Baltimore on September 13-14, 1814 when it valiantly thwarted British invasion. The sight so moved Francis Scott Key that he penned 'Defence of Fort M'Henri', which later became our national anthem ‘The Star-Spangled Banner’. Thus, Fort McHenry claims an irreplaceable spot in our nation's heart.

Fort McHenry welcomes visitors year-round except for Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day. Admission fees apply per vehicle or individuals entering by bike or foot; however, children under 15 years old enter free of charge. Check their website for specifics on current prices.
