## **Introduction**
Lying comfortably within the boundaries of Fort Story Military Base in Virginia Beach, Cape Henry Memorial commemorates the momentous landing of English settlers who established what was to become Jamestown - the first permanent English colony in North America. This historic site forms part of a larger preservation effort under the umbrella of the Colonial National Historical Park, weaving together vital threads in the fabric of American history.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Cape Henry gets its name from Prince Henry (later King James I), namesake patron for this landmark destination. The memorial marks precisely where Admiral Christopher Newport and his brave crew disembarked from their three small ships; Susan Constant, Godspeed and Discovery, onto virgin land on April 26th, 1607. Intriguingly preserved amidst sandy dunes with unobstructed access to Atlantic breezes, visitors can also spot a pair of impressive lighthouses – old and new – which guarded many mariners since the 18th century against treacherous shoals flanking Chesapeake Bay entrance.
