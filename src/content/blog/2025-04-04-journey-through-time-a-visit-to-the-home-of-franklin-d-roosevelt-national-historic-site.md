## **Introduction**
Nestled along the tranquil banks of the Hudson River in Hyde Park, a quiet town on the outskirts of Poughkeepsie, New York, lies a national treasure beaconing lovers of history and culture. Welcome to the Home of Franklin D. Roosevelt National Historic Site - a place where time seemingly stands still revealing key insights into one of America’s most renowned presidencies.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
The foundation stone for this estate was laid in 1800 by James Roosevelt I, Franklin's father. Known as 'Springwood,' this Federal-style manor house is more than just an architectural marvel; it encapsulates over two centuries worth of American history within its walls. Both birthplace and lifelong sanctuary for President Franklin Delano Roosevelt (FDR), Springwood reflects not only FDR's personal life but also his political journey.

Designed by FDR himself, a unique addition is the Presidential Library & Museum onsite - filled with treasures connecting visitors to pivotal moments from his presidency including iconic fireside chats and World War II artifacts.

The site stretches across 800 acres enveloping pristine woods offering natural serenity blended with cultural heritage beautifully depicted in traditional Roosevelt family gardens designed by their mother Sara Delano Roosevelt.

## **Activities Guide**

Step into FDR's study, a room frozen in time where Roosevelt made critical decisions for America during the thick of World War II. Afterward, immerse yourself in the extensive exhibitions at the Franklin D. Roosevelt Presidential Library and Museum - giving you a profound sense of FDR’s enduring legacy.

Moreover, don't miss the opportunity to explore the serene Roosevelt family gardens and wandering trails unveiling tranquil vistas of the Hudson River Valley. During fall, these trails are highlighted with intense colors making it an unforgettable experience for leaf-peepers.

## **Visitor Information**
The site is open year-round except on Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day. The operating hours range from 9 AM-5 PM depending on the season so do check their official website before planning your visit. Admission fee includes access to all facilities including guided tours.

To intensify your experience, we recommend attending special events organized throughout the year like Roosevelt Reading Festival or Naturalist-led hikes that further uncover this site's rich heritage.
