## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

Kings Mountain has its roots deeply entrenched in American history. On October 7th, 1780, men called 'Overmountain Men' from Appalachia came together with American patriots from several colonies to challenge the British forces at Kings Mountain. In an intense melee that lasted just over an hour, these fiercely determined patriots defeated the British contingent led by Major Patrick Ferguson. This triumph significantly boosted American morale and marked a turning point in the Revolutionary War.

From a geological perspective, this region forms part of the Piedmont - an area characterized by rolling hills dotted with lofty pine trees. Explore dramatic landscapes carved by Mother Nature millions of years ago as you undertake hiking trails that wind through dense forests escorting you to scenic viewpoints atop rugged cliffs.
