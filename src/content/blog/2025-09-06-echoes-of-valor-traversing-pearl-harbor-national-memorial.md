## **Introduction**

The sun-dappled Pacific sparkles under the Hawaiian sun as you trace a path across azure waters towards the sombre silhouette of the USS Arizona Memorial. An ethereal quiet descends - this isn't just another picturesque venue for touristic exploits but a shrine devoted to valor, sacrifice, and American resilience. Welcome to Pearl Harbor National Memorial.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

Pearl Harbor National Memorial is much more than its iconic setting on Oahu's south coast. The site encapsulates collective memories and narratives tightly woven into the fabric of American history. On December 7, 1941, without declaration or warning, Japanese forces conducted a surprise aerial attack on Pearl Harbor thrusting America into World War II.

Today's memorial is home to several key historic sites including the remnants of the USS Arizona - sinking on that fateful day claiming 1,177 lives. Also forming part of the memorial are the USS Oklahoma and Utah Memorials eternally stationed within hallowed harbor waters.

Beyond its controversial past lies a geological marvel. Once home to extensive oyster beds giving rise to its name, Pearl Harbor houses fertile mangroves and wetlands exhibiting native flora and fauna.

## **Activities Guide**

Your visit begins at Pearl Harbor Visitor Center where historical exhibits provide context to your upcoming boat ride across to the USS Arizona Memorial. The center also offers access to additional attractions like Battleship Missouri Memorial, Pacific Aviation Museum and USS Bowfin Submarine Museum & Park.

Next comes a documentary screening that heart-wrenchingly revisits the Pearl Harbor attack. After the film, board a U.S Navy-operated boat and traverse tranquil waters towards the USS Arizona Memorial - an elegant white structure straddling the sunken battleship.

Venture onto Ford Island to explore Battleship Missouri where WWII officially ended. Nearby, Pacific Aviation Museum unveils stories of aviators while USS Bowfin offers submarine insights.

## **Visitor Information**

Pearl Harbor National Memorial is open Wednesday through Sunday from 7 am to 5 pm although USS Arizona boat tours operate till 3 pm only. It's recommended to reserve your tickets online in advance.

Comfortable clothing and sun protection are essential. Keep in mind, bringing bags into the memorial (other than small personal items) isn't allowed but lockers are available for rent at the Visitor Center.

## **Tips for Different Visitors**

The sheer scale of the memorial can be overwhelming, especially for first-time visitors or those with limited time. Prioritize based on interest – history buffs might lean toward the museums, while nature lovers could relish exploring wetlands and coastline.

For families, interactive displays at Pacific Aviation Museum captivate younger audiences while easy pathways accommodate strollers and mobility aids making it a comfortable experience for all ages.

Visitors looking for deeper understanding should consider official audio tours providing engaging narratives about exhibits and sites throughout Pearl Harbor National Memorial.
