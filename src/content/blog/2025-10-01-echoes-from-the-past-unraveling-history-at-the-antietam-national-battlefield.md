## **Introduction**
Nestled near Hagerstown, Maryland, is a swath of land untouched by time. Here, echoes of gunshots still seem to ring out faintly as fields and forests bear silent testament to a day steeped in bloodshed - September 17, 1862. This place is none other than the Antietam National Battlefield, a historical canvas that tells a tale of unity achieved through strife.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
It all began on that fateful day when Union and Confederate forces clashed within these serene landscapes, marking the end of a fierce battle with over 23,000 casualties. The Battle of Antietam remains crucial not only for halting Lee's invasion of Maryland but also for facilitating President Lincoln's Emancipation Proclamation.
