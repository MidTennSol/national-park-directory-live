## **Introduction**
Imagine standing atop a spot where 19th-century pioneers battled geographic challenges as they laid down tracks across rugged terrain, aiming to connect Philadelphia and Pittsburgh. As you breathe in the air heavily laden with stories of human will and ingenuity, welcome to the Allegheny Portage Railroad National Historic Site, an often overlooked gem among America's national parks.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Nestled within Pennsylvania’s scenic mountain ranges, this historic site preserves pieces of a revolutionary transportation system dating back to 1834 - The Allegheny Portage Railroad. This was one of America's earliest railroads and an engineering marvel that marked a milestone in global commerce by bridging travel across the Eastern Continental Divide. Here, freight-laden canal boats were lifted over mountains using funicular inclines operated by large steam engines, dramatically reducing travel time between Philadelphia and Pittsburgh from weeks to merely days.

The park boasts two restored engine houses where you can vividly picture how these magnificent machines played out their roles. Step foot through the Lemon House Tavern door that once welcomed weary travelers along this bustling route; stand in awe before the Skew Arch Bridge that stands as a testament to masterful architectural precision aimed at accommodating horse-drawn wagons.

## **Activities Guide**
Your expedition through this historical tableau isn't just about perusing exhibits; there are abundant activities to keep you engaged. Navigate through the comprehensive Visitor's Center and Museum that impeccably narrates the tale of the portage railroad, offering 3-D models, artifacts and audio-visual presentations.

Outdoor enthusiasts can embark on a journey through six miles of trails shaded by lush vegetation, revealing spectacular views of the Staple Bend Tunnel - America's first train tunnel. The educational Junior Ranger Program keeps young travelers entertained while they learn history in an interactive way.

## **Visitor Information**
