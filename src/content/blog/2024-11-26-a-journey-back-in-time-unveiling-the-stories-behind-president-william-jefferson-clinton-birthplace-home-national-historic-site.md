## **Historical/Cultural/Geographical Background**

Surrounded by towering Southern Pines typical to Southwest Arkansas, this unassuming two-story structure was President Clinton’s first home till he turned four. Built in 1917 by Dr. H.S Garrett in American Queen Anne style architecture, it's decorated with ornamental brackets under extended eaves and has characteristic gable-front and wing configuration.

Clinton's maternal grandparents owned the house during his childhood; it was here that he spent formative years under their nurturing influence before moving to Hot Springs, Arkansas. Rich with anecdotes about young Bill's fascination for learning and fondness for music since early years, your walk around will bring history alive as you envisage him engrossed in books or playing saxophone at this very place.

This birthplace home was designated as a National Historic Site in 2011 to honor its significance to President Clinton's life and American history.
