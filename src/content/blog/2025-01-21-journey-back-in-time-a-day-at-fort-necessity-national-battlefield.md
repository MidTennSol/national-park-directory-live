## **Introduction**
Fort Necessity National Battlefield is not just a park - it's a time portal that takes you back to one of the most crucial periods in American history. Situated in the lush meadows of Fayette County, Pennsylvania, this national treasure offers an intimate encounter with a key event that sparked the French and Indian War - the first worldwide war.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
The park's main attraction is a re-created wooden fort where George Washington surrendered to the French on July 3, 1754, marking a pivotal incident that set the stage for the Seven Years' War. 

Next to the fort is Mount Washington Tavern, an authentic early 19th-century inn once part of the historic National Road. The man-made structures contrast beautifully against lush greenery and rolling hills – remnants of shallow seas that grazed the land eons ago.

## **Visitor Information**
