## **Introduction**
Welcome to a world where cultures, histories, and landscapes converge. The story of the legendary Nez Perce tribe unfolds across four beautiful states - Idaho, Montana, Oregon, and Washington. This isn't just a national park; it's a powerful exploration of America's past spanning over 38 distinct sites. Each site offers a unique chapter in the tale of resilience shown by the Nimiipuu or "the real people," as they refer to themselves; their struggle for freedom is a legacy that echoes till date. 

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**
Nez Perce National Historical Park honors not only the ancestral lands but also the spirit of perseverance displayed by Nimiipuu during their eight-month flight from U.S army in 1877—a flight that spanned over 1,170 miles! It's here where you'll experience how history entwines seamlessly with majestic landscapes sculpted by geological wonders spread across years. From ancient rock shelters to solemn battlegrounds, each destination within this park echoes stories of a culture battling against all odds for survival.  

## **Activities Guide**
There's much more than meets the eye at this extraordinary historical park—the diversity in activities perfectly complements its extensive geography. Visit Spalding Visitor Center in Idaho filled with artifacts and exhibits or explore Big Hole National Battleground Site—a powerful reminder of the tragic battle between Nez Perce and U.S army forces. Hiking enthusiasts can explore Bear Paw Battlefield in Montana, the site where the Nez Perce's flight ended. For a serene experience amongst nature, clear your mind with fishing at Nez Perce Fishing Rocks. This park is not just about history; it's about engaging with it.
