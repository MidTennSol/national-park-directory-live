## **Introduction**

IT'S TIME for an adventure at Richmond's crown jewel, permeated by rich history - the Maggie L Walker National Historic Site. Home to one prominent African American woman who changed the course of 20th-century America, this spot offers a deep dive into history that is bound to captivate hearts and spark curiosity.

## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

Let us open the door to the past! Nestled in Virginia's bustling cityscape, the house that was once home to *Maggie Lena Walker* stands tall as a symbol of her resilience and progressive thinking. As the first female bank president (regardless of race) in the United States, Walker’s life is a testament to overcoming societal boundaries.

Walker’s Victorian-style house, originating from 1904-1934, adorns Jackson Ward neighborhood (heart of “Harlem of the South”) like an old-world charm amidst modern structures. Mingling grand architectural details with historical significance marks this site as a cultural paradigm—an epitome intrinsic to Richmond's African-American heritage.

## **Activities Guide**

But history isn't all that awaits. The site radiates richness beyond its walls—inviting visitors to engage via interactive tours, educational programs &amp; exhibits underlining Mrs. Walker’s impressive journey.

The guided tours through her meticulously preserved home offer glimpses into her everyday life. Each room echoes memories highlighting her roles as a businesswoman, social activist, community leader - giving you firsthand encounters with history.

Moreover, the site hosts an on-going, free-to-attend exhibition at the visitor center, named "Yours for Race and Country". This showcases photographs &amp; artifacts from Walker's life—the tangible echoes of her struggle and triumphs.

## **Visitor Information**

Located in Richmond downtown, this national site offers its treasures to residents &amp; tourists alike. Open from Tuesday through Saturday - 9:00 am to 5:00 pm - it requires no entrance fee. It’s paramount, however, to reserve spaces for guided tours in advance due to its overwhelming popularity. 

The visitor center features restrooms and a bookstore where you can purchase related literature or souvenirs that carry the spirit of Maggie Walker.

## **FAQs**

**1. Is photography allowed?**
Photography is allowed for personal use. However, tripods, monopods, and external light sources are not permitted.

**2. Are pets allowed in the historic site?**
Pets are not permitted inside the buildings but can be walked on leashes in the outdoor areas.

**3. Who would find this place interesting?**
The site is perfect for anyone interested in historical figures, African-American heritage, women’s achievements or banking history.

**4. Is the historic site wheelchair accessible?**
Yes, both the visitor center and main floor of Walker's house are handicapped accessible.

**5. Are there any guided park ranger tours available?**
Indeed! Guided park ranger-led tours are available and generally last about 45 minutes.
