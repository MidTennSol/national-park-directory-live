## **Introduction**

Housed in the heart of the bustling city of Boston, Massachusetts, lies a treasured emblem of African American history - the Boston African American National Historic Site. This site serves as an oasis of tranquility amidst urban chaos while carrying forward compelling stories of freedom, courage, and determination. Here you can walk down memory lanes of one of America’s most significant eras and experience history through tangible vestiges.

## **Activities Guide**

The Fletcher Home at 1805 Beacon Street greets visitors with collections reflecting everyday life from an era bygone. As you stroll along its trail, be sure to pause at historic Abiel Smith School – the first public school for black children – or visit Africa Meeting House which has played host to prominent abolitionists like Frederick Douglass.

Free guided tours led by rangers exhibit treasures such as sculptures signifying long strides towards freedom, or murals illustrating courageous tales in riveting detail. For self-guided explorers, there is an interactive mobile application providing detailed information in an engaging manner.

## **Visitor Information**

Being located in a bustling city, this historic site has easy accessibility by public transport with parking options nearby. Though the freedom trail is open year-round, the visitor center (Fletcher Home) operates from Mid April through November. There are no entrance fees for visitors wishing to take the self-guided tour. However, if you require a ranger-led tour, it's advisable to book ahead of time especially during peak season.

## **Tips for Different Visitors**

Wheelchair accessibility is only provided partially due to its location on a hilly terrain; hence, making necessary preparations is recommended for visitors with mobility issues. Parents with children can engage them through scavenger-hunt-style activities available at the visitor center. As historical narratives can get intense, they're better suited for older kids and adults. Photographers will find delight capturing architectural details or picturesque urban sceneries.
