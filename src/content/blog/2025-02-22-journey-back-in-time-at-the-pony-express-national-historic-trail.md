## **Historical/Cultural/Geological Background**

Once used by young and brave riders to deliver mail between Missouri and California over 150 years ago, Pony Express trail extends 1,800 miles through some of America’s most stunning terrains. It epitomizes a vital period of America's growth during Westward Expansion era when connectivity determined survival and prosperity.

The trail weaves past old station sites where daring horsemen once changed steeds during their tireless dash across scorching deserts, over snow-capped mountains and through endless prairies.
